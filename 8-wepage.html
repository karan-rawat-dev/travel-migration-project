From the Past

From being mentioned in ancient religious texts and epics of India to modern day history books - the rich past, heritage and traditions of
Uttarakhand are widely read. Here, monuments of historic and religious importance date back to various years in history - reinstating the
fact that the state indeed has a history of its own, both ancient and medieval. Apart from what can be learnt from history and existing
texts, the land also abounds in local legends, mythology and folklore that have been passed down through generations. An amalgamation of
the written and oral tales makes this state all the more heavenly. Some of the most popular destinations steeped in local lore and mythology
include Bageshwar, Binsar and Lohaghat.
